Octane & Engine Guide
  • Super 98 (closest PH match: RON 100 / Blaze): high-compression, turbocharged and performance engines, or any car whose manual asks for 98.
  • Special 95 (PH: RON 95 / Premium Unleaded): the standard choice for most sedans, crossovers and SUVs.
  • E-Plus 91 (PH: RON 91 / Regular Unleaded): fleet and commercial vehicles, or cars whose manual explicitly allows 91.
  • In UAE desert heat and heavy loads, a grade below your manual's requirement can cause knocking. Always follow the owner's manual.
